A lunar survival game running on real NASA JPL sky data

You run a small robotic lander near the Moon's south pole. The Sun charges your battery, the cold drains it, and you can only send science home when Earth is above the horizon. Every sunrise, sunset and Earth-set in this game comes from NASA JPL's real ephemeris for the dates you play.
